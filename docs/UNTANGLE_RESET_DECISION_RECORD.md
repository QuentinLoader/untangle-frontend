# Untangle South Africa Reset — Locked Decision Record

Status: APPROVED
Date: 2026-10-07

## Brand hierarchy

- **AddVision** is the company and technology owner.
- **Untangle South Africa** is the consumer document-understanding portfolio.
- **TaxSnap, LeaseCheck, PolicyCheck and WorkCheck** are specialist products within the Untangle South Africa portfolio.
- Formal consumer branding should use **Untangle South Africa**. In-app shorthand may use **Untangle** where the South African context is already clear.
- Specialist product presentation should favour:
  - **LeaseCheck — part of Untangle South Africa**
  - **TaxSnap — part of Untangle South Africa**
  - and equivalent wording for PolicyCheck and WorkCheck.
- Corporate trust/footer wording may use: **Untangle South Africa is an AddVision product.**

## Product direction

1. Primary audience remains younger South Africans, while the product must remain credible and useful to any South African consumer or small-business user dealing with important documents.
2. Untangle South Africa remains one platform: one account, one subscription relationship, one Vault/history, one authentication system and one backend.
3. TaxSnap, LeaseCheck, PolicyCheck and WorkCheck are specialist customer experiences with their own acquisition journeys and result experiences.
4. The validated backend analysis engines are preserved. This reset is not a rebuild of the document-analysis platform.
5. Every customer result must support three depths:
   - **30-second answer**
   - **What this means for you**
   - **Full report / evidence**
   Follow-up **Ask** is available where the product contract safely supports it.
6. AI may simplify, explain, summarize and answer grounded questions. AI must not invent facts, legal rules, financial calculations, policy cover, deadlines or unsupported conclusions.
7. Education is a product function. Explain concepts at the point where they matter to the user.
8. Mobile-first means excellent mobile usability, not forcing complex analysis into a single narrow card stack. Desktop and tablet may use a professional report layout with persistent navigation/context.
9. Public marketing and educational content may be accessible without authentication. Customer documents, results, Vault/history, account data, Ask, reminders and admin functions must be protected according to the security contract.
10. The current R79 Untangle Plus monthly offer remains unchanged during this reset. A future one-off detailed report can be researched later; it is not part of this reset.
11. PolicyCheck P01-P04 remain preserved and inactive. Do not activate PolicyCheck or begin P05 customer UI implementation until the new UX architecture is approved.
12. Avoid information repetition. A fact, amount, deadline or warning should have one clear primary home and be repeated only when repetition materially helps the user take action.
